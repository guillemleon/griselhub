## 🇬🇧 English (Authoritative)

### 1. Who We Are

Crescia is a personal finance planning application developed and operated by Crescia ("Developer", "we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Contact: crescia.info@gmail.com
Country: Spain

### 2. Data We Collect

**Financial Data**
All financial data you enter in the App is stored on your device and, if iCloud is enabled, synced to your private iCloud account: monthly income (salary), savings targets, fixed recurring expenses, variable monthly expenses, daily expense transactions (amount, date, note, category), piggy bank goals, and savings deposits and withdrawals. This data is entirely self-reported by you; the App has no connection to any bank or financial institution.

**App Preferences**
We store your selected currency, language preference, and theme mode on your device and in your iCloud account (so they survive reinstallation and sync across your devices).

**Local-Only Data**
Certain flags and dates are stored locally on your device via iOS standard storage (UserDefaults): whether initial setup has been completed, the date the app was last opened (used solely to determine whether to refresh daily budget data), and notification preferences.

**Data We Do Not Collect**
We do not collect names, email addresses, device identifiers, advertising IDs, location data, contact lists, camera data, biometric data, or any data that personally identifies you. The App does not require any account creation or login.

### 3. How We Use Your Data

| Purpose | Data used | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Providing the App's features | All financial data | Contract performance |
| Syncing across your devices | All financial and preference data via iCloud | Contract performance |
| Sending budget notifications | Current month data (local only) | Consent |

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, analytics, or sale to third parties.

### 4. Third-Party Services

**Apple iCloud / CloudKit (Apple Inc.)**
If iCloud is enabled on your device, your financial data and preferences are synced via Apple's CloudKit service to your private iCloud account. This data is stored on Apple's servers, encrypted in transit and at rest, and is accessible only through your Apple ID. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy (apple.com/privacy).

**Apple App Store (Apple Inc.)**
All in-app subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple's handling of payment data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

**Apple Push Notification service (APNs)**
Budget reminder notifications are delivered via APNs. We do not use third-party push notification services.

**No analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting SDKs** are included in the App. We do not use Firebase, Google Analytics, or any third-party data collection services.

### 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Your financial data is stored only on your device and in your private iCloud account (if enabled). We have no access to your iCloud data. We may disclose data if required by law or legal process.

### 6. Data Retention

Your data is stored on your device for as long as the App is installed. If iCloud sync is enabled, data persists in your iCloud account even after uninstalling the App, and can be managed through iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Storage. Uninstalling the App removes all local data (UserDefaults and on-device database). To remove iCloud data, disable iCloud sync for Crescia or delete the data from your iCloud storage settings.

### 7. Your Rights (GDPR and Equivalent Laws)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with data protection laws, you have the following rights:

- **Access:** All your data is stored on your device and in your iCloud account — you have direct access at all times.
- **Rectification:** Edit any data directly within the App.
- **Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Delete your data by removing it within the App, uninstalling the App, and/or removing Crescia data from your iCloud storage.
- **Portability:** Your data is stored locally on your device and can be accessed through standard iOS backup mechanisms.
- **Withdraw Consent:** Withdraw notification consent at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → Crescia.

Since we do not collect or store your data on our servers, most data subject requests can be fulfilled directly by you through the App or iOS Settings. For any questions, contact us at crescia.info@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

### 8. Data Security

All data synced via iCloud is encrypted by Apple in transit (TLS) and at rest. On-device data is protected by iOS's built-in security mechanisms, including device encryption when a passcode is set. We do not operate any servers that store your data.

### 9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 16 and over in the EEA, and 13 and over in other jurisdictions. We do not knowingly collect data from children below these ages. If we become aware that a child has used the App, we recommend the parent or guardian uninstall the App and remove the data from iCloud.

### 10. Push Notifications

With your permission, Crescia sends local push notifications including daily budget reminders and no-expense reminders in the evening. You can enable or disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → Crescia. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device; no notification data is sent to our servers.

### 11. International Data Transfers

If iCloud is enabled, your data may be stored on Apple's servers outside your country of residence, in accordance with Apple's data processing practices and applicable data protection frameworks. We do not independently transfer any data internationally, as we do not operate any servers.

### 12. Widget Data

The Crescia home screen widget displays limited financial data (today's spending, daily budget, available balance). This data is stored locally on your device in an App Group shared container and is not transmitted to any server.

### 13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the App. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Policy.

### 14. Contact and Data Protection

For privacy questions:
Email: crescia.info@gmail.com
Country: Spain
