## 🇬🇧 English (Authoritative)

### 1. Who We Are

Crescia is a personal finance planning application developed and operated by Crescia ("Developer", "we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Contact: crescia.info@gmail.com
Country: Spain

### 2. Data We Collect

**Account Data**
When you register, we collect your first name, last name, and email address. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple may provide your name only on the first authorisation; for subsequent sign-ins only your Apple identifier is used. If you use Google Sign-In, Google provides your name and email address.

**Financial Data**
All financial data you enter in the App is stored in your account: monthly income (salary), savings targets, fixed recurring expenses, variable monthly expenses, daily expense transactions (amount, date, note, category), and savings deposits and withdrawals. This data is entirely self-reported by you; the App has no connection to any bank or financial institution.

**App Preferences**
We store your selected currency, language preference, and theme mode locally on your device via iOS standard storage (UserDefaults / AppStorage). We also store a flag indicating whether you have completed the onboarding flow, and the date the app was last opened (used solely to determine whether to send budget notifications).

**Device Data**
We do not collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, location data, contact lists, camera data, or any biometric data.

### 3. How We Use Your Data

| Purpose | Data used | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Providing the App's features | All account and financial data | Contract performance |
| Sending budget notifications | Last app open date, current month data | Legitimate interest / Consent |
| Account authentication | Name, email, auth tokens | Contract performance |
| Improving the App | Anonymised app usage patterns (see §4) | Legitimate interest |

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

### 4. Third-Party Services

**Firebase Authentication (Google LLC)**
Used to manage your account credentials and sign-in. Your email and name are stored in Firebase Auth. Firebase is a Google service subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

**Firebase Firestore (Google LLC)**
Your financial data (budgets, expenses, savings, categories) is stored in Firebase Firestore, a cloud database operated by Google. Data may be stored on Google's servers within the EU or internationally depending on your Firestore region configuration. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

**Google Sign-In (Google LLC)**
If you choose to sign in with Google, Google provides your name and email to authenticate you. Google's use of that data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

**Apple Sign-In (Apple Inc.)**
If you choose to sign in with Apple, Apple authenticates you and may provide your name on first authorisation. Apple's privacy practices are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy (apple.com/privacy).

**Apple App Store (Apple Inc.)**
All in-app subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple's handling of payment data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

**No analytics or advertising SDKs** are included in the App. We do not use Firebase Analytics, crash reporting services, or any advertising networks.

### 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data only with the third-party processors listed in §4, who process it on our behalf under data processing agreements. We may disclose data if required by law or legal process.

### 6. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, your data will be permanently removed from Firebase Firestore and Firebase Auth within 30 days. Local device data (preferences stored in UserDefaults) is removed when you uninstall the App.

### 7. Your Rights (GDPR and Equivalent Laws)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with data protection laws, you have the following rights:

- **Access:** Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
- **Rectification:** Correct inaccurate data directly within the App or by contacting us.
- **Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
- **Portability:** Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Restriction:** Request that we restrict processing of your data.
- **Objection:** Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
- **Withdraw Consent:** Withdraw notification consent at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → Crescia.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at crescia.info@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

### 8. Data Security

All data transmitted between the App and Firebase is encrypted using TLS. Firebase Firestore encrypts data at rest. We implement access controls so that each user can only access their own data. However, no system is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

### 9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 16 and over in the EEA, and 13 and over in other jurisdictions. We do not knowingly collect data from children below these ages. If we become aware that a child has provided data without parental consent, we will delete it promptly.

### 10. Push Notifications

With your permission, Crescia sends push notifications including daily budget reminders (9:00 AM) and no-expense reminders in the evening. You can enable or disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → Crescia. We do not use third-party push notification services; notifications are sent directly via Apple Push Notification service (APNs).

### 11. International Data Transfers

Your data may be stored on Google Firebase infrastructure outside your country of residence, including in the United States. Google LLC participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses to ensure adequate data protection.

### 12. Widget Data

The Crescia home screen widget displays limited financial data (today's spending, daily budget, available balance). This data is stored locally on your device in an App Group shared container and is not transmitted to any server independently.

### 13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the App or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Policy.

### 14. Contact and Data Protection

For privacy questions, data access or deletion requests:
Email: crescia.info@gmail.com
Country: Spain
